Cregen Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026
Cregen ("we", "us", "our") is an AI video and voice studio app operated by Credstacks Technologies. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Cregen, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: email address, username, and password or sign-in details (for example, Google sign-in).
  • Content you create or upload: text prompts, videos, images, audio, voice recordings, captions, and edits.
  • Posts and interactions: videos you publish, remixes, comments, reports, and blocks.
  • Messages you send us for support.

Information collected automatically

  • Device information: device model, operating system, app version, language, and a device or advertising identifier.
  • Usage information: features you use, generation history, credits used, crashes, and performance data.
  • Approximate location based on your IP address. We do not collect precise GPS location.

Payment information

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your full card details. We receive confirmation of your purchase and your plan status.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the app: generate videos and voices, edit, save, and publish your content.
  • To run features such as remixing, prompt breakdowns, search, and recommendations.
  • To manage your account, credits, and purchases.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, detect abuse, and enforce our rules.
  • To fix bugs, measure performance, and improve Cregen.
  • To contact you about your account, support requests, and important updates.
  • To meet legal obligations.

3. AI processing

To create and analyze content, we send your prompts, uploaded media, and related settings to AI service providers that generate video, voice, and text. They process this data only to provide the service to us, under their own terms and privacy commitments. Please do not upload content you do not have the right to use, or private information about other people.

4. Voice and likeness

You may only upload or clone a voice or face that is your own or that you have clear permission to use. We may remove content that uses a real person's voice or likeness without consent, and we may suspend accounts that do so.

5. Public content

Videos, prompts, and remixes you choose to publish may be visible to other users and may be remixed according to the settings you choose. Content you keep private is not shown to other users. Anything you make public may be copied or shared by others outside our control.

6. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that host our servers, store files, process AI requests, provide analytics, and handle crash reporting (such as cloud hosting and AI service providers).
  • Google Play, for purchases and app distribution.
  • Other users, only for content you choose to make public.
  • Authorities or other parties when required by law, to protect safety, or to enforce our rules.
  • A successor, if Cregen is part of a merger or sale.

7. Retention

We keep your information while your account is active and as long as needed to provide the service. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data and content within 30 days, except where we must keep some information for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Access and edit your account information in the app.
  • Delete content you have posted at any time.
  • Delete your account and data in the app under Settings > Account > Delete account, or by emailing us at info@credstacks.com.
  • Control permissions such as microphone, camera, and storage in your device settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, such as accessing, correcting, deleting, or exporting your data, or objecting to certain processing. Contact us to use them.

9. Children

Cregen is not intended for children under 13 (or under 16 where required by local law). We do not knowingly collect information from children below that age. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, such as encryption in transit and access controls. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, where data protection laws may differ. We take steps to protect it as described in this policy.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version with a new "Last updated" date and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
